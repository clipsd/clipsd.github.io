Screenshot/photo from phone: how to use it

A screenshot or a photo from your phone opens in the ClipSD editor on your computer at once. Nothing to install on the phone, no internet: everything goes over your home Wi-Fi.

  1. On the computer. Click the ClipSD icon near the clock and choose “Screenshot/photo from phone”. A window with a QR code opens. If the computer has a firewall on, press “Allow” once (Ubuntu) or confirm the Windows window.
  2. On the phone. Point the camera at the QR code and open the link. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Tap “Take a photo” (after the shot tap “OK” in the camera) or “Choose a screenshot or photo”.
  3. Send. Tap “Send and finish” or “Send and take another”: the photo opens in the editor on the computer at once. After every photo the reception stays open for 3 more minutes so you can take the next one.

Good to know

  • The computer shrinks the photo to the size of its screen by itself. For the full size, tick “Save photos in full size” in the window.
  • The metadata of the picture (for example, where it was taken) is not kept on the computer.
  • The photo travels over the home network without encryption, so use this at home where the Wi-Fi has a password, not in a café or a hotel.
  • Photos that were not sent stay on the phone for up to an hour: if the connection closed, open “Screenshot/photo from phone” on the computer again, scan the new code with the same phone and the same browser, and the page offers to send them.

Not connecting: what to do

Find in the table what you see and do the one or two actions on the right.

What you seeWhat to do
The page does not open, the browser shows ERR_TOO_MANY_RETRIES or ERR_LOCAL_NETWORK_PERMISSION_MISSING (phone with Android 17)Settings → Apps → your browser → Permissions → “Nearby devices” → Allow, then scan the code again. Chrome 158 and newer asks “Connect to nearby devices?” by itself: tap “Continue” and turn it on. Chrome and Edge 157 do not ask.
The page loads for a very long time or “Site can’t be reached”Turn off the VPN or a blocker (for example AdGuard) on the phone for a minute, or allow “local network access” in it.
The same with Wi-Fi onCheck the network on the phone: the name must be the same as on the computer. A guest network or mobile internet will not do: if the phone switched to mobile data, turn it off for a minute.
It does not open on any network, and the computer runs Ubuntu or WindowsUbuntu: in the window press “Couldn’t connect?”, then “Allow” and enter the password. Windows: “Allow again”, and the network must be “Private”. Another firewall or protection: allow incoming connections to ports 8765-8767 from the home network.
A VPN is on on the computer itselfIt usually does not interfere. If nothing helped, turn it off for a minute.
iPhoneNothing to allow, Safari and Chrome work. If it does not open, check Wi-Fi and VPN.
The phone says “Reception closed” or “Reception time ended”The code expired (3 minutes without a phone, 3 minutes after the last photo). On the computer press “Show a new code” and scan the code.
“Code already open on another phone”Another phone took the code. Press “Show a new code” and scan it with the right phone.
“The window took all the photos”20 photos at a time. Press “Show a new code”.
“Photo too large”Over 25 MB. Shoot at a normal size or choose another picture.
“HEIC format”The computer cannot open it. In the camera settings turn on the most compatible format (JPEG) or choose another picture.
“The computer could not open photo N”Take that photo again.
“The connection to the computer is closed”, photo not sentThe photo is kept on the phone for up to an hour. On the computer open “Screenshot/photo from phone”, scan the new code with the same phone and browser and tap “Send”.
None of this helpedClose the window, open “Screenshot/photo from phone” again and scan a new code. Check that the phone and the computer are on the same network.
